# Office of the Head of State

A NationStates advisor dashboard: it reads your nation, region and RP threads, and a council of AI advisors (Google Gemini) briefs you, ranks the militaries in your RPs, builds dossiers on every nation and drafts your in-character replies.

**Use it here:** https://ns-advisor.pages.dev/

## Getting started
1. Open the app. Settings opens on first run.
2. Enter your nation's NationStates name and your own Gemini API key (free from aistudio.google.com), press **Load models**, and Save.
3. Add **+ New file** for your nation, your region and each RP thread (forum links work).
4. Press **Request new reports**. After that it refreshes itself every 15 minutes while open.

## Privacy
- Everything you enter (your key, notes, files and dossiers) stays in your own browser. It is never sent to the app's owner.
- The app only talks to NationStates (public data), Google Gemini (with your key), and a small proxy that fetches public forum.nationstates.net pages.
- To move to another device, use **Settings › Export setup** and **Import setup**. Your key isn't included; enter it again on the new device.
